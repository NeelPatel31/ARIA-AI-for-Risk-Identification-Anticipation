# News: Landslide Buries Haul Road at Sulawesi Nickel Site

- Event Type: Natural Calamity
- Location Affected: Sulawesi, Indonesia
- Material Affected: Nickel
- Date: 2026-07-28
- Summary: Heavy overnight rain triggered a landslide that buried a primary haul road connecting an open-pit nickel mine to processing facilities in Bahodopi District, the Morowali Regency Disaster Mitigation Agency confirmed Tuesday. Operator PT Sumber Baru Mining suspended ore transport for what officials estimate will be a 10-14 day clearance and slope-stabilization effort. "The slope failed across nearly 80 meters of roadway, and we are not moving equipment back in until geotechnical surveys are complete," a mitigation agency engineer told Antara News. The mine had already been operating under the government's domestic-processing mandate, meaning ore already at the surface cannot simply be rerouted to an alternate port, compounding the delay. Regional stockpiles are reported at roughly two weeks of buffer supply for downstream smelters. No injuries were reported, but three access routes to nearby villages remain closed. This is the second weather-related disruption to Sulawesi nickel logistics this year.
- Estimated Impact: High — compounds Nickel's already Restricted export status and limited stock position with a direct extraction-side outage
