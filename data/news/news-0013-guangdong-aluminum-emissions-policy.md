# News: Guangdong Orders Emissions Cuts at Aluminum Smelters

- Event Type: Government Policy
- Location Affected: Guangdong, China
- Material Affected: Aluminum
- Date: 2026-07-24
- Summary: The Guangdong Provincial Ecology and Environment Department has ordered aluminum smelters across the province to cut energy consumption by 15% under an expanded "dual-control" policy targeting both total energy use and carbon intensity, effective this quarter. Provincial officials said smelters failing to meet interim targets by September could face production curtailments of up to 20%. "We are prioritizing compliance over output in facilities that have repeatedly missed efficiency benchmarks," a department official told Nanfang Daily. The policy is regional, applying only within Guangdong, and does not affect aluminum operations in other provinces. Trade associations estimate the measure could reduce provincial aluminum output by roughly 60,000 tonnes over the compliance period, with smelters likely to prioritize existing contracts over new orders. Several manufacturers sourcing aluminum sheet for solar and bicycle components said they are reviewing supplier diversification within China as a precaution, though no shortage has yet materialized.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate — regional output constraint in Guangdong, not a nationwide Aluminum shortage
