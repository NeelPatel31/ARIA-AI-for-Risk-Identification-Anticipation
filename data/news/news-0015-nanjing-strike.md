# News: Strike Over Overtime Pay Halts Nanjing Turbine Component Line

- Event Type: Labor Dispute
- Location Affected: Nanjing, Jiangsu, China
- Material Affected: Copper, Zinc, and Neodymium components processed in Nanjing
- Date: 2026-07-30
- Summary: Roughly 400 workers at a Nanjing facility processing copper, zinc, and neodymium components walked out Wednesday after management proposed converting mandatory overtime pay to compensatory time off, according to the Nanjing Precision Metals Workers Association. The union said the change would effectively cut take-home pay by 12% for line workers who rely on overtime during peak production months. "Workers have accepted delayed raises before, but not a pay cut disguised as a scheduling change," a union representative said. Plant management has not commented publicly beyond confirming that "operations are temporarily reduced." Local labor mediators have been called in, with both sides expected to resume talks by the weekend. Output of neodymium magnet assemblies, a comparatively low-volume but high-value product line, has reportedly been hit hardest, while copper wire processing continues at roughly 60% capacity using non-striking staff.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate — localized to the Nanjing facility, with the Neodymium line most affected; expected to resolve within 1-2 weeks
