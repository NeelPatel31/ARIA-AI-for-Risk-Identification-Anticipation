# News: Rail Bridge Strikes Threaten Nationwide Metal Shipments

- Event Type: War/Conflict
- Location Affected: Ukraine (nationwide)
- Material Affected: Copper, Zinc, and Aluminum components sourced from Ukraine
- Date: 2026-08-09
- Summary: State rail operator Ukrzaliznytsia confirmed that two of five major Dnipro River rail crossings sustained structural damage from strikes overnight, prompting a nationwide speed restriction on freight traffic while engineers assess the remaining bridges. The State Emergency Service of Ukraine said repair crews would prioritize the crossings carrying the highest freight volume, with full restoration "not expected before the end of the month." In the meantime, Ukrzaliznytsia is capping freight weight limits at 60% of normal capacity system-wide, a move a spokesperson called "necessary to avoid further failures while we inspect the network." Copper wire, zinc ingot, and aluminum sheet shipments bound for western manufacturing hubs are among the cargo most affected, with some shippers reporting week-long delays already. Industry groups warn that continued restrictions could force manufacturers to draw down existing warehouse stock faster than it can be replenished.
- Estimated Impact: High — nationwide capacity constraint affecting all Ukraine-sourced Copper, Zinc, and Aluminum shipments
