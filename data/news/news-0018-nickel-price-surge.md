# News: Nickel Prices Surge on London Metal Exchange After Supply Warnings

- Event Type: Economic/Price Shock
- Location Affected: Commodity-wide (Nickel), originating from Indonesian supply concerns
- Material Affected: Nickel
- Date: 2026-08-19
- Summary: Nickel prices jumped 9% on the London Metal Exchange this week, the sharpest single-week move in over a year, after traders cited compounding supply warnings out of Indonesia including reduced ore shipments and processing bottlenecks. Battery-grade nickel sulfate, used in electric vehicle battery variants, saw an even steeper premium widen against standard-grade metal. "The market is pricing in a real chance that processed nickel supply tightens further before it loosens," one LME floor trader told Metal Bulletin. Analysts note the rally follows weeks of already-elevated volatility tied to Indonesia's domestic-processing requirements and recent logistics disruptions at extraction sites. Battery manufacturers with nickel-variant contracts are reportedly renegotiating price-adjustment clauses rather than absorbing the full spot increase, while several are said to be accelerating qualification of alternative chemistries that reduce nickel content. Exchange data shows warehouse inventories at a 14-month low.
- Estimated Impact: High — commodity-wide price shock compounding Nickel's Restricted export status and limited stock position
