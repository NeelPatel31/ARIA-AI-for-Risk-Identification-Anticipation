# News: Safety Grievance Triggers Work Stoppage in Bila Tserkva

- Event Type: Labor Dispute
- Location Affected: Bila Tserkva, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine
- Material Affected: Aluminum and Zinc components processed in Bila Tserkva
- Date: 2026-08-11
- Summary: Workers at an aluminum and zinc processing plant in Bila Tserkva refused to enter the facility Monday morning after a ventilation system failure was reported but not repaired over the weekend, the Bila Tserkva Metalworkers Trade Union said. Union representatives claim air quality readings inside the fabrication hall exceeded safety thresholds for two consecutive shifts before management acknowledged the fault. "We asked for an inspection, not a shutdown, but management left us no choice," a union organizer told regional outlet Kyiv Post Ukraine. Plant management said an independent inspector has been called in and expects to reopen within 48 hours pending results. Roughly 150 workers are affected, and aluminum sheet output for solar panel components has been paused entirely during the stoppage, while zinc processing continues at a reduced rate using a secondary line unaffected by the ventilation fault.
- Estimated Impact: Low to Moderate — localized to Bila Tserkva, expected to resolve within days pending inspection results
