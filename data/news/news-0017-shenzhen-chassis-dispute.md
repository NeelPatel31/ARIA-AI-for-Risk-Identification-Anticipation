# News: Contract Dispute Slows Shenzhen Laptop Chassis Line

- Event Type: Labor Dispute
- Location Affected: Shenzhen, Guangdong, China
- Material Affected: Titanium, Copper, and Aluminum components processed in Shenzhen
- Date: 2026-08-16
- Summary: Production of titanium, copper, and aluminum chassis components slowed to roughly half capacity at a Shenzhen facility this week after the Shenzhen Electronics Manufacturing Workers Federation organized a slowdown over stalled contract renewal talks. Workers say a promised skills-based pay tier, negotiated a year ago, has not been implemented despite repeated requests. "We are not walking out, we are working to the letter of our current contract, nothing more," a federation spokesperson said, describing the action as a deliberate slowdown rather than a strike. Plant management disputed the characterization, telling Shenzhen Business Daily that output figures reflect "routine seasonal maintenance," though the union released internal shift logs suggesting otherwise. Talks are scheduled to resume Friday. Buyers relying on the plant's titanium and aluminum chassis lines report shipment delays of roughly one week so far, while copper wiring output has been less affected due to cross-training among unaffected staff.
- Estimated Impact: Low to Moderate — localized to one Shenzhen facility, with Titanium and Aluminum lines most affected
