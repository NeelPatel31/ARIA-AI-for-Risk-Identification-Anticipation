# News: Drone Strikes on Black Sea Corridor Disrupt Nationwide Freight

- Event Type: War/Conflict
- Location Affected: Ukraine (nationwide)
- Material Affected: Steel and Polyester components sourced from Ukraine
- Date: 2026-08-02
- Summary: A wave of overnight drone strikes targeting port infrastructure near Odesa has forced the Ukrainian Sea Ports Authority to suspend loading operations along the southern export corridor, the Ministry of Infrastructure said Sunday. Under an emergency measure dubbed "Operation Coastal Shield," inbound and outbound freight is being rerouted through inland rail to Lviv and Kyiv, adding an estimated 6-9 days to transit times nationwide. "Every export route we have is now running through a single rail bottleneck," a ministry logistics coordinator said in a televised briefing. Marine insurers have reportedly widened war-risk exclusion zones along the coast, raising freight premiums for any cargo touching southern ports. Manufacturers dependent on steel billet shipments and polyester textile exports report order backlogs building nationwide as trucking capacity is diverted to military logistics. No timeline has been given for restoring port operations.
- Estimated Impact: High — nationwide delays across Steel and Polyester shipments regardless of originating city
