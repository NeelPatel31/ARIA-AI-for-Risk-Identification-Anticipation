# News: Pearl River Overflow Floods Guangzhou's Panyu Industrial Corridor

- Event Type: Natural Calamity
- Location Affected: Guangzhou, Guangdong, China
- Material Affected: Aluminum, Zinc, Silicon, Copper, and Titanium components processed in Guangzhou
- Date: 2026-07-14
- Summary: Sustained monsoon rainfall pushed the Pearl River more than two meters above its warning level overnight, flooding low-lying sections of Guangzhou's Panyu Industrial Corridor. The Guangzhou Municipal Flood Control Office ordered four multi-product assembly plants in the district to halt operations Monday after pump stations were overwhelmed. "We have never seen water rise this fast in this district," a control office spokesperson told local broadcaster GZTV, noting drainage crews had been redeployed from neighboring Foshan. Officials estimate a 4-7 day shutdown while electrical systems are inspected for water damage. Because Panyu hosts solar-panel, bicycle-frame, and steel-furniture component lines in close proximity, the disruption is unusually broad for a single flood event, touching aluminum and zinc stamping as well as silicon, copper, and titanium finishing lines. Provincial rail freight into Guangzhou has been rerouted through Dongguan, adding an estimated two days to inbound raw-material deliveries.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate to High — concentrated in Guangzhou but affecting an unusually wide span of materials due to co-located plants
