# News: Forest Fire Forces Precautionary Shutdown at Stryi Metalworks

- Event Type: Natural Calamity
- Location Affected: Stryi, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine
- Material Affected: Steel and Iron components processed in Stryi
- Date: 2026-07-21
- Summary: An unusually dry July has fed a fast-moving forest fire in the Carpathian foothills that reached within three kilometers of Stryi's metalworking district by Tuesday evening. The Stryi Municipal Emergency Service ordered a precautionary shutdown of steel and iron processing lines, citing smoke inhalation risk and the diversion of the town's water supply to firefighting efforts. "Our priority is containment before the wind shifts toward the plant corridor," a service official said in a briefing carried by regional outlet Lvivska Hvylya. The Carpathian Foothills Forestry Authority reports roughly 600 hectares burned so far, with containment lines holding along the ridge above the industrial park. Local officials expect a 3-5 day suspension of frame and component processing, with rail deliveries of raw iron ore from Kryvyi Rih being held at a siding outside Stryi until the shutdown lifts. Neighboring Lviv's steel operations are unaffected and continuing at normal capacity.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate — localized to Stryi's steel and iron processing, expected to resolve within a week
