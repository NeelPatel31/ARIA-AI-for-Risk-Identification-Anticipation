# News: Ukraine Sets National Export Quota on Zinc

- Event Type: Government Policy
- Location Affected: Ukraine (nationwide)
- Material Affected: Zinc
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Summary: Ukraine's Cabinet of Ministers approved a decree capping zinc exports at 70% of the prior year's volume, citing the need to build a strategic materials reserve amid ongoing wartime disruptions to import channels. The Ministry of Economy said the quota applies to all zinc leaving Ukrainian territory regardless of destination, effective the first of next month. "We cannot allow critical inputs for domestic infrastructure rebuilding to leave the country unchecked," a ministry official said in a statement carried by Ukrinform. Exporters will need to apply for allocation certificates through a new online portal, with priority given to long-term contract holders over spot-market shipments. Foreign buyers of Ukrainian zinc, including several European solar-component manufacturers, have reportedly begun inquiring about alternative sourcing to hedge against reduced allocations. The decree includes a review clause after six months, with officials signaling the quota could tighten further if reserve targets are not met.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate to High — nationwide export constraint on Zinc affecting all buyers sourcing from Ukraine
