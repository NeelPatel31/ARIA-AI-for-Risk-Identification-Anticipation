# News: Copper Prices Jump as Smelter Cutbacks Tighten Global Supply

- Event Type: Economic/Price Shock
- Location Affected: Commodity-wide (Copper), originating from smelter output cuts in Chile and Asia
- Material Affected: Copper
- Date: 2026-08-04
- Summary: Copper prices climbed 7% on the London Metal Exchange this week after three major smelters announced output reductions totaling an estimated 8% of global processing capacity, citing surging electricity costs and declining ore grades. "We are looking at the tightest refined-copper market since 2021," a London desk head told Metal Bulletin, noting registered warehouse stocks sit at a two-year low. The Antofagasta Metallurgical Union confirmed its Chilean facility would run at 80% capacity through the quarter pending an energy-supply review. Electronics, solar, and wind-turbine assemblers buying copper wire and sheet under fixed-price contracts report no immediate volume shortfall, but several say they are re-opening price-adjustment clauses ahead of schedule. Analysts warn that any fresh demand uptick ahead of the Q4 buying season could extend the rally, mirroring recent pressure in other battery-adjacent metals.
- Estimated Impact: Moderate to High — commodity-wide price pressure on Copper, a shared hub material for Smartphone, Solar Panel, Wind Turbine, and Laptop
